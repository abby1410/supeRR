Pressure is leverage

A delivery is high pressure when its possible outcomes would swing the batting side's win probability the most. That single measure combines the asking rate, wickets in hand, balls left and how close the game is.

Share of pressure balls by over

First innings: early wickets in the powerplay and the death overs carry the most leverage. Chase: pressure builds towards the finish.

The verdict from split halves

We split the matches into two random halves 40 times. A real clutch skill would show up in both halves. Among regulars it does not.

True spread (SD) of the pressure-specific effect, in net runs per 100 balls, by how much data a player needs to be included. The signal at the loosest threshold comes from part-timers, whose role differences look like pressure skill.

How much of each measure is skill

True SD = between-player spread that repeats across halves. k = how many balls (or matches) of evidence it takes before a player's own record outweighs the league average.
